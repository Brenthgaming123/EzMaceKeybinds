# Reporting bugs

Found a bug? Open an issue on GitHub and pick **Bug report**:

https://github.com/Brenthgaming123/EzMaceKeybinds/issues/new/choose

## What to include

- Your Minecraft version
- Your loader (Fabric or Quilt)
- The mod version
- Which keybind you pressed and what you expected to happen
- Your latest.log or crash report, if you have one

## Before you report

- Check the FAQ and the Known issues page.
- Check that your items are in the hotbar and the keybind is set.
