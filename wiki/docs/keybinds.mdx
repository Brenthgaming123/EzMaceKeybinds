# Keybinds

## Trigger Air Pot
Throws your splash potions (swiftness, healing, strength) and then uses a wind charge. Needs a wind charge in your hotbar.

## Trigger Elytra/Armor Swap
Swaps between your elytra and a chestplate from your hotbar.

## Trigger Firework
Uses a firework rocket.

## Trigger Wind Charge
Uses a wind charge.

## Trigger Pearl
Throws an ender pearl.

## Trigger Pearl Catch
Throws an ender pearl, then uses a wind charge. Needs both in your hotbar.

## Trigger Lunge Swap (1.1)
Swaps to your spear, attacks, then swaps back.
