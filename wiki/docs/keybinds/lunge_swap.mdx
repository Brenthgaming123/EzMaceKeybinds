# Trigger Lunge Swap

Swaps to your spear, attacks, then swaps back.

**Available in the 1.1 builds only** (Minecraft 1.21.11 and 26.x).

## What you need in the hotbar

- A spear

## What it does

1. Swaps to the spear in your hotbar.
2. Attacks.
3. Switches back to your original hotbar slot.

If you are already holding a spear, it just attacks.
