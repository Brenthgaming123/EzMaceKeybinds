# Trigger Elytra/Armor Swap

Swaps between your elytra and a chestplate from your hotbar.

## What you need in the hotbar

- An elytra and a chestplate

## What it does

- If you are wearing an elytra, it swaps to a chestplate from your hotbar.
- Otherwise, it swaps to an elytra from your hotbar.

## Swap/Revert mode

A separate keybind, **Elytra Armor Swap/Revert Mode**, toggles between Swap mode and Revert mode. An action-bar message shows which mode you switched to. Revert mode is the default.
