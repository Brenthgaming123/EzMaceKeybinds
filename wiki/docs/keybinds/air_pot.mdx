# Trigger Air Pot

Throws your splash potions and then uses a wind charge, all in one press.

## What you need in the hotbar

- A wind charge (required)
- Splash potions of swiftness, healing and strength (any of these that you have)

## What it does

1. Throws the splash potions it finds in your hotbar.
2. Uses the wind charge.
3. Switches you back to your original hotbar slot.

If there is no wind charge in your hotbar, nothing happens.
