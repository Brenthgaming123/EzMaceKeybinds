# Introduction

Struggling to do Mace techniques? This could be your chance to get good like Mace PvPers!

Ez Mace Keybinds is a client-side Fabric mod that turns the fiddly hotbar juggling behind mace and elytra PvP into single keypresses.

## What it does

- Air pot: splash potions and a wind charge in one press
- Elytra and chestplate swap, with a Swap/Revert mode toggle
- Firework and wind charge triggers
- Ender pearl throw and pearl catch
- Lunge Swap for spears (1.1 builds only)

Every keybind is **unbound by default**, so nothing interferes with your controls until you set them up.

The items you use must be in your **hotbar** (slots 1 to 9). If an item is missing, the keybind does nothing.
