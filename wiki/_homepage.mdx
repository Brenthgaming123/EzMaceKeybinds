Struggling to do Mace techniques? This could be your chance to get good like Mace PvPers!

Ez Mace Keybinds is a client-side mod that turns the fiddly hotbar juggling behind mace and elytra PvP into single keypresses. Air pot, swap between elytra and chestplate, fire fireworks, pearl catch, throw pearls, use wind charges and lunge swap, all with just keybinds.



![Divider](@ezmacekeybinds:divider.png)





![Features](@ezmacekeybinds:features.png)



- **Trigger Air Pot:** throws your splash potions (swiftness, healing, strength) and then a wind charge
- **Trigger Elytra/Armor Swap:** swaps between your elytra and a chestplate from your hotbar
- **Elytra Armor Swap/Revert Mode:** a keybind that toggles between Swap and Revert mode, with an action-bar message
- **Trigger Firework:** uses a firework rocket
- **Trigger Wind Charge:** uses a wind charge
- **Trigger Pearl:** throws an ender pearl
- **Trigger Pearl Catch:** throws an ender pearl, then uses a wind charge
- **Trigger Lunge Swap** (1.1): swaps to your spear, attacks, then swaps back



![Divider](@ezmacekeybinds:divider.png)





![Setup](@ezmacekeybinds:setup.png)



Every keybind is **unbound by default**, so nothing interferes with your controls.

1. Open Options, then Controls, then Key Binds.
2. Scroll to the **Ez Mace Keybinds** category.
3. Bind each action to the key you want.

**Important:** the items you use must be in your **hotbar** (slots 1 to 9). The mod looks for them there, and if an item is missing the keybind does nothing. After a sequence it switches you back to your original slot.

![FAQ](@ezmacekeybinds:faq.png)



**The keybind does nothing. Why?**

- Check that the keybind is actually set, since they're unbound by default.
- Check that the items it needs are in your hotbar, not deeper in your inventory.
- Check that you downloaded the file that matches your Minecraft version.

**Which file should I download?**

- Pick the file for your Minecraft version. For 1.21.11 there is a 1.1 build with Lunge Swap. For 1.21 to 1.21.10 use the 1.0 build, which has no Lunge Swap. For 26.x use the 26.x build, which needs Java 25.

**Does it work on Quilt?**

- It should, since Quilt loads Fabric mods. Use Fabric API. It hasn't been fully tested on Quilt, so please report any problems.

**Does it work on Forge or NeoForge?**

- No. It's a Fabric mod and has no Forge or NeoForge build.

**Do I need to install it on the server?**

- No. The mod is client-side only.

**Is it allowed on servers?**

- It automates item swapping and use in timed sequences, so check a server's rules before using it on multiplayer.

**Can you port it to another Minecraft version?**

- Open a **Version Port** issue on GitHub. Versions lower than 1.21 won't be ported.
